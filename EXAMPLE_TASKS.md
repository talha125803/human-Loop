## Task Characteristics

All tasks in Humanity Loop are:

✅ **Safe** - No dangerous or illegal activities
✅ **Meaningful** - Focus on real personal growth
✅ **Achievable** - Realistic time commitments
✅ **Impactful** - Create positive change
✅ **Respectful** - Consider others' boundaries
